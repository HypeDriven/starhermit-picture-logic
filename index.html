How to play

Every puzzle hides a pixel picture. The numbers beside each row and above each column are run clues: they tell you the lengths of the filled runs in that line, in order. A row clue of “2 1” means a run of two lit cells, at least one empty gap, then a single lit cell.

Light cells

Tap or click a cell in Fill mode to light it. A wrong fill counts a mistake and shows you the cross instead.

Cross empties

Switch to Mark mode (or press X) to cross cells you know are empty. Crosses are notes — they never count as mistakes.

Finish lines

When every lit cell of a line is placed, the board crosses the rest of the line for you. Complete every lit cell to reveal the picture.

Scoring

Points for lit cells, finishing under par, and clean boards. Mistakes cost 50, hints cost 25. A flawless board earns +500.
